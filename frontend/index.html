Deterministic Architecture

The 5-Stage Verification & Trust Pipeline

Every product attribute passes through 5 deterministic checkpoints before reaching the export gateway or the human verification queue.

01

SHA-256 & Ingestion

Files (.xlsx, .csv, .pdf) are SHA-256 hashed and validated. Ingestion is strictly idempotent to avoid duplicate records.

Multi-Format Ingestion
02

Dual-Pass VLM Extraction

Two independent passes extract layout-anchored bounding boxes. Discrepancies collapse score to DISPUTE (0.4) for human review.

OCR & BBox Anchoring
03

Physical Gates & UOM

Standardizes SI units (kW, V, °C, mm) with explicit Value & UOM separation. Checks against physical constraints.

Value & Unit Normalizer
04

Sibling SKU Inference

Propagates series attributes to incomplete sibling records, strictly tagging them INFERRED and capping confidence at ≤0.70.

≤0.70 Hard Confidence Cap
05

Trust Routing Gate

Scores ≥0.90 auto-approve to Unilog .xlsx & GS1. Inferred (<0.90) and disputed attributes route to HITL queue.

≥0.90 Auto / <0.90 HITL
