Deterministic Architecture

The 5-Stage Verification & Trust Pipeline

Every product attribute passes through 5 deterministic checkpoints before reaching the export gateway or the human verification queue.

01

Ingest & Hash

PDFs and CAD exports are SHA-256 hashed on arrival. Ingestion is strictly idempotent so re-runs never duplicate records.

Input: PDF / Image / Part #
02

Dual-Pass VLM

Two independent passes extract layout-anchored bounding boxes. Discrepancies collapse score to DISPUTE (0.4) for human review.

Qwen2-VL / llama.cpp
03

Deterministic Normalizer

Standardizes units (kW, V, °C, mm) and checks against physical constraints (e.g. max voltage bounds, IP rating enums).

Physical reality check
04

Sibling SKU Inference

Propagates verified series attributes to incomplete sibling records, strictly tagging them INFERRED and capping confidence at ≤0.70.

Knowledge Graph
05

Trust Routing Gate

Scores ≥0.90 auto-approve to GS1 CSV and JSON-LD. Inferred, borderline, and disputed attributes route to the HITL dashboard.
